# Adjective `:Tính từ`
## Phân loại
- Descriptive adjectives `:Miêu tả`
    + Ex: good, golden, dry
- Demonstrative adjectives `:Chỉ định`
    + Ex: this, that, these, those
- Indefinite adjectives `:Bất định`
    - Quantitative adjectives `:Số lượng`
        + Ex: some, much, (a) few, many, (a) little, any, no, all, one
    - Distributive adjectives `:Phân bổ`
        + Ex: each, every, either, neither, any
- Numeral adjectives `:Số từ`
    + Ex: one, two, third, tenth
- Interrogative adjectives `:Nghi vấn`
    + Ex: which, what, whose
- Possessive adjectives `:Sở hữu`
    + Ex: my, your, his, her, its, our, thier
- Articles `(có thể xem là tính từ):Mạo từ-mang nghĩa "là một"`:
    + Ex: a, an, the
## Cấu tạo
1. Tính từ đơn
    - noun + "y": rain+y=>rainy;cloudy
    - noun + "ly": day+ly=> daily; manly
    - noun + "ful": care+ful=>careful;beautiful;
    - noun + "less": care+less=>careless; homeless;
    - noun + "en": gold+en=>golden;woollen
    - noun/adjective + "ish": girl+ish=> girlish; bookish; whitish
2. Tính từ ghép
    - adjective + adjective: a dark-blue coat
    - noun + adjective: snow-white cotton
    - ...
